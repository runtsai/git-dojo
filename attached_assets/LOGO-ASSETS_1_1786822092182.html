Clear space and placement

Clear space: keep free space around the seal equal to at least the height of the “BRED” cap on all sides. For the wordmark, keep at least one cap height above and below the rules.

Garment placement (per the launch spec): seal on the left chest at 3.5–4″ wide; BRED WITH PURPOSE stacked on the back at 11–12″ wide, first line 2.5–3″ below the collar seam. Ink: Ink #23281D on Sandstone; Cream #F5E6C8 on the greens.
